Review Studio selected and regrouped the original surfaces, aligned axes,
scaled the complete scenes, assigned illustrative colors, and converted them
to GLB. The derivative meshes and their preview renders share the same license.

Urinary tract source meshes: FJ3129–3130, FJ3144–3149.
Vascular source meshes: FJ3145, FJ3147, FJ3427, FJ3441, FJ3659,
FJ3476–3477, FJ3576–3577. The descending aorta is supplied as a continuous mesh.
Only the main renal arterial and venous trunks are shown.

The organ surfaces show exterior relationships. They do not depict the
internal collecting system, renal fascia or sex-specific variants.

Nephron and collecting duct
Original Review Studio teaching schematic. Segment order follows REN 123
renal histology and nephron teaching; geometry and proportions are illustrative.
The model omits microvascular and fine epithelial detail. One nephron connection
is shown; a collecting duct receives flow from more than one nephron.
