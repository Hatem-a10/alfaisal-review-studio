# REN 123 source intake

The REN 123 timetable controls current dates and group assignments. The supplied past-question booklet is recalled material. Its labels are indexed by topic because older lecture numbers can differ from the current timetable. Source PDFs and presentations are served only to signed-in students through the existing private asset route. The repository stores source titles, stable IDs, checksums, byte counts and locators.

The cards are introductory source-guided checks. Full authored explanations, per-objective graded questions, and a scored Renal mock or booklet mode remain editorial work. The recalled booklet was indexed using its contents page. Its individual printed keys, figures and question numbering were not independently audited or converted into a scoring bank. Image-dependent questions retain their original page context.

The ten staged objects were read back from private staging R2 and matched by SHA-256. The production bucket requires the same ten approved objects and readback verification before release. Existing CVP files, IDs, results and review timestamps remain intact.
